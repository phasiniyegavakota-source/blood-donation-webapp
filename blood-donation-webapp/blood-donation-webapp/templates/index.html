How it works

1. Register

Donors sign up with their blood group, contact info, city, and last donation date so the system can track eligibility automatically.

2. Search

Recipients or coordinators search by blood group and location. Donors who donated within the last {{ 90 }} days are automatically excluded to reflect real donation eligibility rules.

3. Connect

Submit a blood request and immediately see a list of currently eligible matching donors to reach out to.
